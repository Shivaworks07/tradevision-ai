Kill switch
Immediately halts auto-trading across every broker credential you have connected. Existing open orders on the broker are not cancelled — this only stops new signal-triggered orders. An execution already in progress at the exact moment you click this may still reach the exchange — this stops new submissions, it cannot recall a network request already underway.
